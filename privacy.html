What we collect, and why.

This notice explains what personal information we receive through this website and when you contact us, how we use it, and your rights.

Last updated 10 October 2026

Who we are

Rhanamerzy Concepts Limited is a company registered in Nigeria (RC 1709197). We are responsible for the personal information described here.

You can write to us at Rhanamerzyconcepts@yahoo.com, in English or Chinese, or call +234 813 365 7572 or +234 706 525 3636.

What this website collects

This website uses no cookies, advertising or analytics.

It saves your language choice in your browser, on your device, so that the site opens in your language next time. M, the digital guide, remembers which tips it has shown until you close the tab. You can clear both in your browser’s settings.

What you type to M stays on your device. It is not sent to us or saved.

The company that hosts this website keeps technical records of each visit, such as your IP address, approximate location and browser. It uses them on our behalf, to deliver the pages and keep the site secure.

When you contact us

When you email or call us, we receive the details you choose to give us. These are usually your name, organisation, email address and telephone number, your country or market, the kind of enquiry and your message.

The contact form on this site asks for the same details. Sending from the form is coming soon. Until then, it sends nothing.

How we use your information

We use it to reply to you, to discuss the work you ask about, and to keep a record of our dealings with you.

We rely on these legal grounds:

  • you have asked us to act, and we need your details to take the steps you request before any agreement
  • our legitimate interest in answering enquiries and running our business
  • a legal duty, where the law requires us to keep records

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for marketing unless you agree. We do not make decisions about you by automated means alone.

Who we share it with

We share it only with:

  • the services that run our email, messages and this website for us
  • our professional advisers, where needed
  • public authorities, where the law requires it

Some of these services store information outside Nigeria, for example in the United States. When information leaves Nigeria, we protect it as the Nigeria Data Protection Act requires.

How long we keep it, and how we protect it

We keep your information only as long as we need it for your enquiry, for any work that follows and for our legal duties. Then we delete it.

We take reasonable steps to protect it from loss, from misuse and from anyone who should not see it.

Your rights

You can ask us:

  • whether we hold information about you, and for a copy of it
  • to correct it, or to delete it
  • to stop using it, or to use it only in limited ways

If you have given your consent to something, you can withdraw it at any time.

To use these rights, write to us at Rhanamerzyconcepts@yahoo.com. We may ask you to confirm who you are first.

If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission at ndpc.gov.ng (opens in a new tab).

If you are in China, the European Union or another place with its own data protection law, you may also have rights under that law. Write to us about them.

Other things to know

This website is for organisations and the people who represent them. It is not meant for children.

It links to other websites, such as the Mercy Alexander Foundation’s. Their own privacy notices apply there.

We may update this notice. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
